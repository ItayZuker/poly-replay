# Market

Live trading console for the selected series.

## Trade controls

| Control | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Allow trade** | Off = demo; on = real orders when other gates pass. Control lives under [Settings](doc:settings) → **User** as an **Off** / **On** switcher. Turning **Off** also moves every Trigger card that was on **Trade** back to **Demo** |
| **Prediction** | Manipulation / prediction detector. Per-series switch; the label shows **· On** / **· Off**. Detects adverse UP/DOWN Buy quotes vs Gap. When **On**, the panel expands to show **Trade**, Order type, Shares, quote/Duration/Shift/Profit inputs, Trigger Area, Buy/Sell quote boxes, and Right/Wrong. When **Off**, that block collapses so only the Prediction switcher and ⓘ remain — ⓘ still opens the short info note (not the settings). **Trade** · On/Off is active only when **Allow trade** and **Prediction** are both On — turning either Off forces Trade Off and disables it. **Settings** sync per series; **triggers and scoring run only on the deployed app** (not localhost). |

Phase **Auto Trade**, **Use Schedule**, chart phase setups, **manual quote orders** (Size / Manual Override / clickable UP·DOWN boxes), and **Prediction Trade** (real Prediction buys/sells) are removed. The **only** live buy/sell path is **Trigger cards** (**Trade** + **Active**, with **Allow trade** on). Prediction, when present, scores in **sim only** (Right/Wrong) — it never places orders.

| Setting | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Trade** | Removed for live orders (always Off). Prediction scoring uses the trigger Ask as the buy basis |
| **Order type** / **Shares** | Legacy Prediction Trade fields — unused for live orders |
| **Max Quote (¢)** | Max price of the cheapening Buy when Duration starts (1–99, default **90**). For Prediction DOWN that is UP Buy; for Prediction UP that is DOWN Buy |
| **Min Quote (¢)** | Min price of that cheapening Buy when Duration starts (1–99, default **70**). Must be ≤ Max Quote |
| **Duration (Sec)** | Seconds the condition must hold (compare now vs that many seconds ago) |
| **Shift (¢)** | Minimum drop of that cheapening Buy over Duration (1–50, default **5**). Example: Max Quote **80**, Shift **5** → must reach **≤ 75¢** |
| **Profit prediction (¢)** | After a Prediction trigger: predicted-side **Sell** (Bid) must reach the **trigger Ask** + this many ¢ sometime before window end for **Right** (1–50, default **5**). Window outcome is ignored |
| **Trigger Area** | Dual-handle bar over the market window timeline. Time labels under each handle move with the dots (`0:00` → window length, e.g. `5:00` for a 5m market). Only the span between the handles is watched |
| **Prediction** | Two half-width controls under Trigger Area (shown when Prediction is **On**): visual **Buy** / **Sell** quote boxes (`—`) — not clickable, never place orders. On trigger: **Buy** locks at the trigger Buy; **Sell** shows live Bid. **Right** when Sell reaches trigger Ask + **Profit prediction (¢)**; **Wrong** at window end. Click ⓘ for the Gap vs UP/DOWN Buy trigger note |
| **Right / Wrong** | Per-series counts updated when each prediction is scored by Profit prediction (including re-triggers after Right and background parked predictions). **Reset** clears both counts for the selected market |

**Trigger:** while Gap stays the same or stronger in its direction, UP Buy gets cheaper and DOWN Buy gets more expensive (mirror for a negative Gap). **On top of that**, when Duration starts the cheapening Buy must be between **Min Quote (¢)** and **Max Quote (¢)**, and over Duration it must drop by at least **Shift (¢)**. On trigger, the price graph container border turns **green** for Prediction UP or **red** for Prediction DOWN, and stays until the window ends. **Score:** Sell (Bid) must reach trigger Ask + **Profit prediction (¢)** anytime before window end — not whether that side wins the market.

Feed **Latency** is shown in the Settings page header. **Fill success** lives under Settings → **Stats**. See [Settings & wallet](doc:settings).

**Available markets**, **Recording**, and per-series **retention** are managed in the separate **Admin CRM** (not in this trader UI). Only available series appear in the market picker; trading APIs reject unavailable series. Market picker labels show **Min** instead of “minutes” (e.g. **Bitcoin - 5 Min**). Recording still runs only on non-`TRADING_EXECUTOR` processes. On the recorder, stalled Chainlink (~20s) discards the active window and reconnects; a silence watchdog covers ~60s with no book+Chainlink (discard + reconnect both feeds + restart recorder) and ~20s CLOB-only silence while Chainlink still flows (reconnect market WS only, keep the window). See [Information flow](doc:data-flow).

Live Trigger Trade: **Allow trade** on, trigger on **Trade** + **Active**. Hour-slot results appear on [Schedule](doc:schedule).

## Chart

Shows PTB, price, and gap. Phase bands / setup editing are removed.

## Quotes, positions, triggers, book, log

Left-column sections (Wallet, Positions, Triggers, Book, Log) share resizable headers — drag a section header to grow or shrink the panels above and below. When the left column is covered, the rail shortcuts reopen Positions, Triggers, Book, or Log. **Allow trade** is on Settings → **User** (not in the left column).

**Mobile (narrow viewports):** the Market page stacks vertically instead of using the center column splitter. Top block order: Buy/Sell Up/Down quote boxes, then the price chart (**200px** tall), then PTB / Current / Gap / Tick underneath the chart. Positions, Triggers, Book, and Log sit full width below that. The Wallet balance always moves into the app header’s first row, to the right of **Poly Replay** (stats sit on the row below). There is no **Connected** label — the balance slot shows the USDC balance when connected, or **No Connection** when not. Tap the balance to refresh; while refreshing, it is replaced by a spinning refresh icon until the update finishes. The whole page scrolls vertically (graph + sections together) with no visible scrollbar. PTB / Current / Gap / Tick and the Buy/Sell Up/Down quote boxes stay in view as two rows of two (no horizontal scroll). The market dropdown row is **market picker → countdown** (countdown right-aligned). **Market** / **Schedule** / **Heatmap** use the same bordered segmented control as desktop (full width in the mobile header nav, equal thirds), with a gap above the header’s normal bottom border. The active segment uses the accent blue fill; inactive segments stay transparent on the header background. The page content below is darker than the header. Positions / Triggers / Book / Log headers are tap-to-expand and tap-to-collapse (no drag resize). Each header shows a chevron on the left that rotates **180°** when that section is open. The column splitter and cover rails are hidden.

- Up/Down quote boxes above the graph are **display-only** (live Ask/Bid; latch when a bot fill locks a quote). The rightmost value is always the live market Buy/Sell quote (outside the scroll). Latched fill prices sit in a **fixed-width** strip to its left that scrolls horizontally (newest on the left; scroll jumps left when a new fill is added; scrollbar handle only). **Up Buy ↔ Up Sell** and **Down Buy ↔ Down Sell** scroll together; when one side has a latch without a matching fill on the other, the other strip shows an empty spacer slot so columns stay aligned. Not clickable — no hover lift, no manual orders
- **Positions** — Bet UP/DOWN cards (buy times in UTC): Trigger **Trade** and **Demo** fills from the **server** trading engine (SSE); the browser does not keep its own Demo card store. Positions cards are stored in Mongo per user × series (**Open** cards plus settled cards from the **last 24 hours**; older settled cards are removed automatically). Cards sit in a responsive **grid** (**min 300px**, columns share remaining space with **1fr**; newest top-left; last-row cards keep the same width); on mobile each card is full width. On page load (and until live cards are ready from the server), the section shows a short loading spinner instead of a partial list so cards do not jump. Demo cards show a **Demo** label left of the card **status** on the first row. If a Trigger buy fill lands outside that trigger’s Ask band (or above Start Shares), the card also shows a **Trigger Miss** label — stats and Sell/hold still follow the trigger setup as usual (no auto-flatten). Each Trigger Trade / Demo card’s first row is the Market Trigger **title** (name at fill time; falls back to the current card title if still in the list) with the market **window range in UTC** in brackets (e.g. **Name (17:05–17:10)**) on the left and the card **status** (Open / Sold / Win / Loss) on the right — window from the card’s window key / slug to match Polymarket’s titled 5m/15m market (ET on their site ≠ UTC on the card). The next row is **Bet UP/DOWN** with **buy time (UTC)** in brackets to its right (same muted style as the window range), and **shares @ buy price** on the far right of that row. Header filter **Demo** / **Trade** / **All** (default **All**) shows only matching cards; the choice is remembered in this browser. **Clear** (right of the filter) removes settled Positions cards for the current filter only — **Demo** clears Demo, **Trade** clears Trade, **All** clears both — and never removes **Open** cards. Clear does **not** change Trigger card Demo/Trade totals, Market P/L, or Schedule hour stats. Deleting a Market Trigger removes that trigger’s **Demo** Positions cards; **Trade** Positions and Real trade history stay. On held settlements, **Settlement** / Win / Loss settle on the same official outcome clock as Prediction (**explicit Gamma** payout prices ~$1 / ~$0 — the same source Polymarket uses to pay tokens; no extra portfolio wait). **Demo** held settles update that buy’s **entry-window** Positions card (not the live rolled window) via the same `/api/window-resolution` Gamma outcome Trade cards use. Demo keeps at most one **Open** Positions card per trigger **per market window** (no second buy in that window until that position closes). After an early exit, the trigger may open again in the same window as a **new** Positions card. Prior-window **Open** Demo cards waiting for settlement are kept when a later window opens — they are not replaced. After a refresh, tab close, or a temporary poll pause, past-window **Open** Demo cards resume that same Gamma settlement on load and when the market window rolls (Win/Loss is never invented without an official outcome). Win/Loss is whether your bet side matched that outcome. A clearly resolved held-token mark (~0 or ~1) can correct a mismatched feed on live Trade cards. Portfolio marks may refine P/L dollars after that, never decide the outcome alone when Gamma is present. Prediction scoring still shows ✓ / ✕ on Trigger Area Sell — it does **not** add cards here
- **Triggers** — created detector triggers for the workspace, scoped **per signed-in user and per market series** (switch BTC/ETH/… and you get that market’s cards only; definitions + Demo mode/pause/demo stats + **display order** in Mongo — Mongo is the source of truth; edit/delete on prod or local and the same cards sync for that market. Deleting a card removes it from Mongo; a stale browser that later tries to update a missing card drops it locally (it does not recreate it — a refresh restores a card only if Mongo still has it). Cards sit in the same responsive **grid** as Positions (**min 300px**, columns share remaining space with **1fr**; last-row cards keep the same width); on mobile each card is full width. Drag the wide colored left handle (grip icon) on a card to reorder across the grid (order is saved). The section title (**Triggers**) is a refresh control (same icon as Wallet): click to reload Demo stats from Mongo and Trade stats from the server when a Positions card settled but card totals look stale. **Demo** + **Active** is scored on the **production trading host** only (not localhost, not in the browser tab): FAK/FOK fills wait for the live Settings **feed Latency (ms)** (**Buy GTD** / **Sell GTD** do not), Positions cards and Demo stats update with the browser closed, and opening the app only **reflects** server cards/stats (no second client scorer). **Trade** + **Active** still places real orders on that host when **Allow trade** is on. **Demo** + **Active** cards trigger **independently** (several may be open at once so you can compare setups). **Trade** + **Active** cards **race**: the first buy owns the window until that position sells or the window ends; then Active Trade cards race again. Each card shows its **Title**, a **Demo** / **Trade** switcher (default **Demo**), a **Pause** / **Active** toggle (default **Pause**), then a visual-only **BUY** / **SELL** row (side by side when space allows; stacks to a column with the same wrap as Demo/Trade and Pause/Active; not clickable): BUY/SELL highlight is stored on the trigger in Mongo (Demo and Trade) and updated by the trading host — on a buy the left **BUY** cell highlights green (UP) or red (DOWN) with side, price, and shares; on an early sell the right **SELL** cell shows for **5 seconds** (then clears), or clears sooner if a new buy opens or the market window ends; held-to-settlement clears BUY without a SELL flash. Localhost picks up the same highlight on refresh from Mongo; live updates stream on the production host. Below that: green/blue/red dots (**Sell** / **Win** / **Loss**), then a **Stop Loss** row with a right-aligned P/L value (colored background chip, no “P/L” label; wraps to its own row with the same spacing when the card is narrow), and a **⋮** menu with **Edit** / **Duplicate** / **Replay** / **Delete**. **Replay** opens the Open Replay popup for that trigger’s **Demo** Positions windows only (Open + last-24h settled Demo cards; Trade fills are omitted); the title shows a **DEMO WINDOWS** label with the trigger name. **Duplicate** adds a copy with a new id (same definition otherwise), empty Demo stats, **Paused**, **Demo**, and a **new random** handle color — inserted just below the source card. **Create Trigger** also assigns a random handle color (editable in the dialog). In **Demo**, a reset icon (same as Live header stats) sits on the right of the green/blue/red stats row and clears that card’s Demo stats (confirm first). Demo Win/Loss/Sell/Stop Loss counts are credited once per settled Demo Positions card (same card cannot double-count after retries). **Trade** mode has no Reset — live totals stay. Green = **Sell** (profitable early exit); blue = **Win** (held to window end and won); red = **Loss** (held to window end and lost). **Stop Loss** counts early exits at a loss (including stop-loss sells and fee-losing early sells). Switching **Demo** / **Trade** also switches which stats the card shows: **Demo** stats are stored on the card (Mongo); **Trade** stats are the all-time live totals from the server, credited only when a real Trigger Trade position card settles the same way as phase cards (sold fill or official held outcome) — not from client-side guesses. **Pause** (vs **Active**) stops evaluation entirely and, if the card was on **Trade**, switches it back to **Demo**. Only when **not paused**: **Demo** simulates fills and updates Win/Loss/Sell/Stop Loss/P/L on the card; **Trade** places real orders only on the production trading host (requires **Allow trade**), and records **Trade** Win/Loss/Sell/Stop Loss/P/L on the server (Mongo) from that same settled position card (sold fill or official held outcome — not a client-side flat guess). Localhost can edit cards and run **Demo**, but does not place Trigger Trade orders. Turning **Allow trade** Off moves every **Trade** card to **Demo**. If Trade is selected while Allow trade is already off (or the host cannot execute), the card stays on Trade but does not place orders or collect Demo stats. Schedule **Live** lists Market Triggers in the left column and shows hour-slot Trigger Trade stats on the week grid. **Trigger Trade** fills attribute to the UTC hour cell covering that window (Schedule hour cells + Schedule total) when the trigger was Trade+Active at that time (Active/Paused timeline). Schedule **Replay Triggers** stay browser-local and are separate from Market Triggers. **Create Trigger** opens the dialog; **Create** (requires a **Title**) adds a **Paused** / **Demo** card. Dialog tabs: **BUY** / **SELL** / **Stats**. **BUY** holds the duration diagram with two fixed vertical bars. Absolute **Price** / **Range** bars use a **0.0–100.0¢** scale in **0.1¢** steps (bottom = 0.0, top = 100.0; labels always show one decimal, e.g. **50.1¢**). Both bars are **BUY** (Ask) only — there is no SELL Price quote mode. **Left (start):** **UP/DOWN Range** (min/max ¢ band, two dots; min band width **0.1¢**) or **UP/DOWN Price** (one dot on that absolute scale); UP/DOWN Range arms when Ask rounds to a 0.1¢ value inside the band, UP/DOWN Price when Ask equals that price at **0.1¢** precision. **Right (end):** **UP/DOWN Range** (same 0.1¢ absolute scale) or **UP/DOWN Change** (signed whole ±¢ from Duration start — e.g. +20 rise at least 20¢, −50 fall at least 50¢, **0** = unchanged). **Duration 0** disables the right bar and the right half of the center diagram (end price + end gap + trend wheel) — only the left **UP/DOWN Range**/**UP/DOWN Price** and left gap apply, and the trigger fires immediately with no wait. Below **Apply window** on the BUY tab: **Share** (1-100000, default **10**) then **Type** is `FAK` / `FOK` / `GTD` (default **FOK**). Click the ⓘ icon or the **Type** label to expand FAK / FOK / GTD explanations under the row (collapsed by default). **GTD** is enabled only when Duration is **0**, the left bar is **UP/DOWN Price**, and **no +/− Gap** is set on either side (any gap forces FAK/FOK). With those conditions: it posts **two** resting limit buys at that ¢ (one UP and one DOWN) without waiting for a quote/tick, starting at **Apply start − Offset GTD placment** (wall clock; **Offset GTD placment** sits to the right of the **Apply window** label — same value+unit control as Duration; default **0** = place at Apply start; enabled only when Buy **Type** is **GTD**, otherwise disabled with muted colors). Offset may place before market-window open (e.g. Apply at `0:00` and offset **5 min** on a 5m market) or earlier inside the window (e.g. Apply at `2:00` and offset **30 sec** → place at `1:30`). **Feed latency does not delay Buy GTD** (resting limit — Demo fills as soon as Ask is at or below Price on either side; tries UP then DOWN until a fill succeeds). FAK/FOK Demo buys still wait for feed latency. The server accepts at most **one** GTD rest per side per trigger per market window (failed place calls may retry); once a side is accepted it is not re-placed that window. When either rest fills, the other side is cancelled. If a Buy GTD is still resting when the trigger leaves its place window (past Apply end, or the card is **Deleted** / **Paused** / moved to **Demo**), that rest is **cancelled** (including rests placed before the window opened). After a GTD fill the card does **not** re-arm while still holding; it rearms only after a **confirmed full exit** (sell / flat) in a later window. Holding to settlement (Take Profit and/or Stop Loss at **100**) also keeps the GTD from placing again in that window. Same rules apply in Schedule **Replay Triggers**. Borders stay green for BUY. A gold dashed **PTB** line always runs mid-height across the diagram. A placeholder zigzag market-price line appears only when a gap is set on at least one side. A **Fixed** / **Relative** control sits centered between the two top gap buttons (one mode for the whole trigger; disabled until at least one gap button is active). **Fixed** (default): top/bottom gap buttons set an optional absolute market-vs-PTB requirement — top **+ Gap** = market above PTB (green fill), bottom **- Gap** = market below PTB (red fill). **Relative**: the same buttons become **Gap With BUY** / **Gap Against BUY** — gap direction is checked against the BUY side that matched the Ask condition (Ask/price first, then gap). **Gap With BUY** = UP needs +gap and DOWN needs −gap; **Gap Against BUY** = UP needs −gap and DOWN needs +gap. Min/Max gap size still applies (gold, same as the Relative gap buttons — not green/red). Relative mode does **not** show the green/red zigzag/fill graph (With/Against are not absolute +/− colors). In **Fixed**, the zigzag sits above or below the mid PTB line for that half; enabling the same gap direction on the other half continues on that same side without moving the first half. Only opposite start/end directions make the line cross PTB mid-span. Each active Fixed half’s band between PTB and the price line fills and the price line is colored the same red/green; both sides active cover the full width. Each active gap has **Min** / **Max** + a dollar size (0 = any gap size): in Fixed, on the opposite side of the PTB line from the gap fill; in Relative, always above the PTB line. Clicking the active gap button clears that side. When **both** halves use the **same** gap side (+/+ or −/− in Fixed; Gap With BUY on both, or Gap Against BUY on both, in Relative), a **trend wheel** appears on the PTB mid-point: spin the two-sided handle (or type the center value) for the signed **market-price change over Duration** in dollars, with **Min $ Change** / **Max $ Change** centered under the wheel for +/+ or With/With, or above the wheel for −/− or Against/Against — on top of the gap-size filters. Example: **+20 Min $ Change** = asset must rise at least $20 from Duration start; **−40 Max $ Change** = asset must fall, by at most $40. Default is **Min $ Change** at **0**, which has no effect. The zigzag slopes to preview that change (larger |$| = steeper). The wheel is hidden when gaps are unset or on opposite sides. Left bar = start UP/DOWN Range or UP/DOWN Price; right bar = end UP/DOWN Range or UP/DOWN Change. A timeline runs along the bottom between the bars; the right end has a duration value + unit (**ms** / **sec** / **min**), and **0** means no Duration (immediate fire on start). Buys use the selected **Buy order type** (**FOK** = full size or no fill; **FAK** may partial-fill) only when the best Ask is still **inside** the buy diagram band — **at or above** the band low and **at or below** the band high for whatever ¢ range the user set (end Range; or the start UP/DOWN Price / start Range when Duration is **0**, Buy **GTD**, or end mode is UP/DOWN Change). If Ask has moved outside that band by fire/send time, the buy is skipped. Size is **Start Shares** (share-capped): the limit is placed at the live Ask inside the band (not at the band high as a dollar budget), so fills stay at most that many shares (FAK may be fewer). If a fill still lands outside the band or oversized, the position is kept, Sell/hold follow the trigger setup, stats count normally, and the Positions card shows **Trigger Miss**. Below that, **Apply window** is a horizontal bar with two draggable dots for the portion of the market window when the **Buy** may arm/fire (same 0→window-end time scale as Prediction **Trigger Area**; defaults full window). After a buy fills, **Sell** setup (TP/SL / held) runs through **market-window end** regardless of Apply end. **SELL** has **Sell order type** `FAK` / `FOK` / `GTD` (default **FAK**; click the ⓘ icon or label to expand the same style of explanations under the row), plus **Take Profit (¢ from fill)** and **Stop Loss (¢ from fill)** (1–100 each; defaults **10** / **10**) — Bid must rise or fall that many ¢ from the **buy fill** (not absolute quote prices). Stop Loss is active only when the buy fill (¢) is **greater than** the SL offset (e.g. SL 10 needs buy > 10¢); otherwise SL is ignored and the position stays open for TP or held settlement. **100** disables that exit (never sell on that path); the SELL tab then shows **(Disabled)** next to that field’s muted label. If neither TP nor SL hits before window end, the position is held to the market outcome: **blue** on the Trigger card when the buy side wins, **red** when it loses (green still means a profitable Take Profit exit). If the Trade position goes **flat earlier** (any full exit), the card records that sell with the **actual exit price / P/L** (green or red) — it does **not** later count a held blue/red at $1/$0. With Sell **GTD**, a resting limit sell is placed at **fill + Take Profit** as soon as the Buy fills (does not wait for Bid; skipped when Take Profit is **100**). **Feed latency does not delay Sell GTD** exits; FAK/FOK Demo sells still wait for feed latency after TP/SL is hit. With Sell **FAK**, exits retry until the position is flat; **FOK** retries on later ticks if the order does not fully fill. Stop-loss or window-end with a resting GTD cancels it and sells **FAK**. After a full exit the card may fire again in the same window if conditions rematch. **Stats** has sub-tabs **Demo Stats** and **Live Stats**. Default when opening Stats: **Live Stats** if the card is **Paused** or on **Trade**; **Demo Stats** if **Active** and on **Demo**. **Live Stats** uses all-time server Trade totals; **Demo Stats** uses the card’s Demo totals. Each sub-tab shows a **Total** table (outcomes in order: **Loss** (held to window end and lost), **Sell** (early exit with profit), **Win** (held to window end and won), **Stop Loss** (early exit at a loss), and **P/L**, with column headers **%** (share of Loss+Sell+Win+Stop Loss) and **Count**). Below that, an **Average** table of Active time from the mode timeline (Trade+Active on Live Stats, Demo+Active on Demo Stats): **Weeks** / **Days** / **Hours**, each with **Avg P/L** for that unit (that sub-tab’s P/L ÷ those weeks/days/hours). Runtime (deployed host): while unpaused — **Buy GTD** (Duration **0** + left **UP/DOWN Price** + no Gap) posts two resting limits (UP and DOWN) from **Apply start − Offset GTD placment** by wall clock (Trade, Demo, and Schedule Replay; may be before window open) without waiting for a quote/tick — at most one accepted rest per side that window (failed places may retry); first fill cancels the sibling; rests cancel when leaving the place window or on Delete / Pause / Demo; otherwise when inside **Apply window** an UP or DOWN Ask matches the start **UP/DOWN Range** band or exact start **UP/DOWN Price**, then the optional start gap (Fixed absolute, or Relative Gap With/Against BUY for that side): if **Duration** is **0**, it opens a position immediately (end price/gap/trend ignored); otherwise it watches for **Duration**, then requires the end condition (+ optional end gap for the locked side); on match it opens a position and exits on take-profit, stop-loss, or window end (Sell is not clipped by Apply end). On a **Trade** buy (or a **Demo** buy while **Allow trade** is Off), the price graph shows one filled **dot** at the buy moment (green for UP, red for DOWN — the same single buy/sell dots used for phase and other fills; never a double marker) and a light matching **full-height** Duration band (fill only, no border) covering the Duration window that led to that buy; markers clear when the market window rolls. **Demo** still collects local stats while **Allow trade** is On, but does not draw those graph markers
- **Book** — live CLOB depth for the selected series (same Polymarket book as the UP/DOWN quote boxes). Two columns (left **UP**, right **DOWN**) separated by a vertical divider; each column is labeled **BUY UP** / **BUY DOWN** at the top (asks) and **SELL UP** / **SELL DOWN** at the bottom (bids). One mid line spans both columns at the vertical center of the Book section as you resize it. Up to **10** ask (buy) levels grow upward from that line and **10** bid (sell) levels grow downward (best ask/bid nearest the mid; farther levels clip when the section is short). The current BUY/SELL levels (best ask just above the mid line, best bid just below) show a border — green on UP, red on DOWN. Size and price (¢) per level; updates with the live quote feed. No card/panel background on the ladders
- Log of bot / order activity (Trigger Trade, settlement, Prediction sim scoring)

**Header Market P/L** is the sum of settled trade results for the selected series (fees included), including Trigger Trade and any remaining legacy fills. Historical manual wins/losses still count here and in **Live**, but not on [Schedule](doc:schedule) hour cells. The wallet balance is your current USDC cash — it only matches Market P/L after accounting for deposits/withdrawals, and only once losing tokens are resolved and winning tokens are redeemed into USDC. Polymarket usually auto-redeems wins; worthless losing tokens may still show as “redeemable” dust (~$0). Held Open Positions stay Open until official Gamma Up/Down (~$1 / ~$0); Win/Loss and stats update then. No token-mark / portfolio fallback for window outcome. The trading host hard-polls Gamma for ~20 minutes after window end, then light retries until resolve (same Gamma source as recordings / Open Replay Official).

Fees: when Polymarket’s trade feed includes the USDC notional, the app uses that for an exact fee; otherwise it estimates from the market’s taker fee curve.

Wallet credentials: [Settings & wallet](doc:settings).
